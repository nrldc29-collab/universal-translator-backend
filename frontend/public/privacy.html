Anai Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Anai is a real-time conversation bridge. Audio you speak is processed to provide speech-to-text, translation, and text-to-speech.

What we process

  • Microphone audio during active sessions
  • Transcripts and translations for the conversation
  • Basic connection and diagnostics logs on the server you use

Where processing happens

Self-hosted bridge: audio stays on your PC or server you control.

Anai Cloud: audio is sent to the hosted bridge URL configured for your app build. We do not sell conversation data.

Retention

Session data is ephemeral by default. Operators may enable logging; see your deploy settings.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the project repository or contact your organization administrator.
