Quick tips

  • Tap You speak or They hear language pills to pick from all 14 languages.
  • Tap Link bridge, then Start, and speak naturally.
  • Tap Pause to stop the microphone; tap Start to resume.
  • Use Together so each person speaks their language — Anai bridges meaning both ways.
  • Tap lane ↗ or ⧉ to share or copy text.
  • Tap the status bar when connected for technical details.
  • Use ↗ in the header to share the current session.
  • Tap LIVE in the header or Linked in the dock to disconnect.
  • For slang or strong accents, follow the native speaker listen hint before relying on voice.
  • Tap meaning check chips to switch source language or repeat exact terms.
  • If Wi‑Fi drops, the strip shows reconnect progress; tap Retry if it fails.
