# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and focused on improving Anai Translator.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Give actionable feedback.
- Respect user privacy, security, and local data.
- Avoid harassment, insults, or personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sharing private data, secrets, or generated user audio without permission.
- Harassment or discriminatory language.
- Disruptive or intentionally harmful contributions.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and productive.
